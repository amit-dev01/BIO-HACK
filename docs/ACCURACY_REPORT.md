## 1. Executive Summary

CanopyAI is an automated geospatial platform designed for rapid screening of forest above-ground biomass (AGB) and carbon storage using multi-sensor spaceborne observations (NASA GEDI spaceborne LiDAR, ESA Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery, and Google Dynamic World / ESA WorldCover land classification).

This report evaluates CanopyAI's estimates across **10 primary global forest benchmarks** spanning tropical, temperate, boreal, and mangrove biomes, alongside **3 critical edge-case environments** (hyper-arid desert, pelagic open ocean, and active agricultural deforestation).

## 2. Methodology

### 2.1 Benchmark Selection Criteria
Benchmark polygons (100 to 2,000 hectares) were situated within globally recognized ecological research sites possessing extensive ground-inventory validation records:
- **Tropical Moist Rainforests**: Central Amazon (Brazil), Congo Basin (DRC), Borneo (Indonesia), Yasuní (Ecuador), and Western Ghats (India).
- **Temperate Mixed & Coniferous Forests**: Black Forest (Germany) and Redwood National Park (USA).
- **Boreal Taiga Forests**: Kainuu (Finland) and Central Siberia (Russia).
- **Halophytic Coastal Mangroves**: Sundarbans Delta (Bangladesh).

### 2.2 Operational Satellite Datasets
- **NASA GEDI L4A (v2)**: Footprint-level Aboveground Biomass Density (AGBD) derived from waveform LiDAR ($25\text{ m}$ footprint) collected aboard the International Space Station.
- **Google Dynamic World (v1)**: $10\text{ m}$ near real-time land cover taxonomy (9 classes) driven by deep learning on Sentinel-2 top-of-atmosphere reflectance.
- **ESA Sentinel-2 MSI (Level-2A)**: Surface reflectance for 10-meter Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) and multi-year seasonal trajectory screening.
- **ESA WorldCover 2021 (v200)**: Global $10\text{ m}$ discrete land cover classification.

### 2.3 Evaluation Thresholds
A standard scientific tolerance threshold of **$\pm 40\%$ relative to the published literature midpoint** was applied. This tolerance corresponds to the expected confidence interval of spaceborne LiDAR wall-to-wall mapping when aggregating discrete footprint shots without local destructive calibration plots.

## 5. Scientific Discussion & Uncertainty Analysis

### 5.1 Where CanopyAI is Most Accurate
1. **Intact Tropical Moist Evergreen Broadleaf Forests (Amazon, Congo, Yasuní)**:
   - High GEDI shot density ($> 30$ valid shots per polygon) combined with continuous closed canopy ($> 85\%$) yields reliable LiDAR waveform returns ($RH_{98} \approx 28\text{--}35\text{ m}$).
   - Low seasonal leaf loss ensures Sentinel-2 NDVI remains steady ($> 0.82$), and Dynamic World consistently maps $\ge 90\%$ tree cover.
2. **Managed Temperate Broadleaf & Mixed Forests (Black Forest)**:
   - Clear contrast with surrounding agricultural land; excellent ground-truth calibration from European national forest inventories.

### 5.2 Sources of Remote Sensing Uncertainty
1. **GEDI LiDAR Sampling Density & Orbit Traces**:
   - GEDI operates as a sampling profiler rather than an imaging sensor. Footprints are $25\text{ m}$ circles spaced approximately $60\text{ m}$ along track and $600\text{ m}$ cross-track. Smaller polygons ($< 50\text{ ha}$) can suffer from shot sparsity ($n < 5$).
2. **Persistent Cloud Cover in Equatorial Tropics**:
   - Tropical regions (e.g., Borneo, Gabon, Western Ghats) experience cloud cover up to $80\%$ of the year. CanopyAI validates cloud contamination and penalizes confidence by $-10$ points when cloud cover exceeds $30\%$.
3. **LiDAR Waveform Saturation in Extreme Biomass Forests**:
   - In hyper-dense old-growth stands such as California Coastal Redwoods, above-ground biomass frequently exceeds $600\text{--}1,000\text{ Mg/ha}$. Spaceborne LiDAR waveform energy can attenuate before reaching true ground level in multi-layered crowns, underestimating extreme basal areas unless supplemented with airborne LiDAR.
4. **Steep Topography & Slope Distortion**:
   - Mountainous terrain (e.g., Andes, Himalayas, Western Ghats) broadens LiDAR return waveforms due to elevation differentials across the $25\text{ m}$ footprint, slightly elevating tree height uncertainty.

## 6. Honest Limitations & Scope of Use

1. **Screening-Level Accuracy ($\pm 25\text{--}40\%$)**: CanopyAI is engineered for preliminary investment screening, carbon risk assessment, and deforestation alerts. It is **not** a legal substitute for on-the-ground forest mensuration.
2. **No Soil Organic Carbon (SOC)**: Soil carbon, which often comprises $30\text{--}70\%$ of total ecosystem carbon in boreal and peatland systems, is not measured.
3. **No Dead Wood / Litter Carbon Pools**: Only live above-ground and below-ground arboreal biomass are quantified.
4. **Generic Root-to-Shoot Ratios**: Below-ground biomass (BGB) uses IPCC Tier 1 default ratios by ecozone ($0.21\text{--}0.26$) rather than site-specific root core excavation.
5. **No Direct Carbon Credit Issuance**: Carbon credit issuance under registries like Verra (VCS VM0047/VM0015) or Gold Standard mandates third-party audit validation and rigorous field plot sampling.
